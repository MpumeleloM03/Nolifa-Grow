# The pitch: one proposition, one demo

## The proposition
**Community resilience.** When disease, drought or an outage hits, a community loses food and water first. Nolifa keeps three things working together:

| Part | Problem | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Protect** | Farms lose crops and herds to disease that spreads before anyone knows | Scan a leaf or check a sick animal, see outbreaks on a map, get early warning |
| **Prepare** | Taps run dry with no warning and no way to ask together | Water radar: forecast, community vote, provider response, farm suppliers |
| **Provide** | Food does not reach people when supply chains fail | Local farms sell to the people nearby, with escrow, a delivery PIN and drivers |

Everything else (inspection, plans, proof-backed community, crop tracker) exists to make one of those three trustworthy. The three apps are the same proposition for three people: Grow for the farmer, Drop for the household, Delivery for the driver.

If a judge asks "what is the one thing", the answer is: **a farm and its neighbours stay fed and watered when something goes wrong.**

## The demo: one water disruption, start to finish (3 minutes)
All data is labelled demo. Nothing is faked silently.

1. **Warning.** Open Nolifa Drop. The pop-up says Umlazi water is low (33%), about 4.6 days to critical. The notification says Welcome back and repeats it.
2. **Where it comes from.** Open the Water page, scroll to "Where this comes from": who measures it, how fresh it is, who responds, and what happens if nobody does.
3. **Ask together.** Tap Vote for help. Each account counts once. Show the threshold bar.
4. **Provider side.** Open the provider desk (demo mode, or a provider account). The request has reached the threshold. Acknowledge it, dispatch trucks with an ETA. This is the process a real provider needs to run.
5. **Community sees it.** The Water page and Nolifa Grow's Community alerts show "Trucks on the way".
6. **A farm helps.** In Nolifa Grow, Water provider: a farm with a borehole offers 5 000 L to the same community.
7. **Food keeps moving.** Back in Drop, order bottled water and candles from Nolifa Depot. Pay (simulated, labelled), driver collects, delivers, customer gives the PIN, money is released.
8. **Close.** Trust and Safety lists what was simulated and what is real.

## Extra beats for a longer demo
- **A bad road.** In Nolifa Delivery accept a job. The map shows the route to the farm and the customer. Tap the amber button, tap Flooding. The next driver on that road sees "Flooding in 1.4 km" and is asked "still there?" when close.
- **Water you can trust.** Water page, Real dam levels today: Midmar and the others from the Department of Water and Sanitation, with the date.
- **The AI that waters.** Irrigation AI: one plot is told to water today with the litres and the minutes, another can wait because the soil still holds enough or rain is coming. Tap Why the AI says this.
- **A herd, not just cows.** Livestock: goats, sheep and a horse, each with its own symptoms, a health record and a pin on the herd map.
- **Proof.** Impact evidence: the accuracy of the scanner with its range and sample size, and the forecast checked against the weather that happened.

## What happens when no provider participates
The radar still works as a community tool: readings can come from farms and volunteers, votes stay visible so an ignored request is public, the planner says how many 25 L bottles close the gap, and neighbours and farm suppliers can deliver them. The provider's reply is the part that needs their participation, and we say so.
